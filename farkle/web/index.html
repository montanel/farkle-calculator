About the numbers

Turns to win Average turns to reach the game-points target, playing optimally for it — banking the instant a pass crosses the line. Sets are ranked by this, lowest first, and it already prices in busting.
Wins & busts Open a set for the odds under the same need-aware play, at your target: how often a whole turn banks everything you still need, and the bust rate — high while far from the target, low once the finish is in reach.
Strategy Inside a set, when to throw and when to pass. For most strong sets it is one rule — keep throwing until your tally covers what you need, then pass — with a small per-hand risk of throwing table; weaker sets add the spots where you bank early.
Ordinary dice The game fills any slot you do not fill yourself, so sets using fewer than six special dice are considered automatically.
Caveat Turns to win assumes a solo race to a fixed points target, not a duel with an opponent who might reach the line first.
